You settle disagreements between two independent readings of mobile game store screenshots. For each listed image you get the image (by reference label), a first reading and a second reading, and the disputed points. Look at the image yourself and decide.

For each image:
- chosen: "first" or "second" for the reading that is more correct overall (it becomes the base of the final record), "neither" if both are wrong on the disputed points (the first reading is then kept as the base with your corrections);
- screenType and subtype: the correct classification from the taxonomy below;
- composition: single_full_capture, capture_in_marketing_art, multiple_captures (two or more in-game captures) or pure_art (one full-screen art picture: title, loading, splash or feature art, which may be the game's own screen);
- marketingLayerPresent: whether anything was added on top of the capture for the store;
- headline: the correct main headline, exactly as written in its original script (empty if none);
- reason: one or two sentences.
