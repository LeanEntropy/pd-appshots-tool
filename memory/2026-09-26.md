## Empirical vision-model bake-off done
- Report: `docs/research/vision-models-bakeoff.md`. Scripts/raw outputs in scratchpad `vision-bakeoff/` (temp, not committed).
- 10 images (5 own marketed screenshots + 5 competitor JP/KO/TW/TH) x 5 models, identical prompt/schema. Total spend $2.24 (Claude side); Codex is subscription-billed.
- Claude side: all 4 requested models reachable (opus-5-5, sonnet-5, haiku-4-5-20251001, fable-5-1). GPT side: only `gpt-6-astra` works on this ChatGPT account — probed and rejected gpt-6-astra-mini, gpt-6.1-astra, gpt-5.3-codex, o5, o4-mini.
- Findings: Haiku hallucinates/garbles Korean and Thai text specifically (reads Japanese/Traditional Chinese fine) and twice swapped in-capture UI text for the real marketing headline on our own screens; its latency is bimodal (14-115s on identical flags, unpredictable extended thinking). Sonnet/Opus/Fable/GPT-6-astra all read every JP/KO/TW/TH headline in the sample exactly right. Fable is uniquely good at picking the correct per-screen band colour from behind our shared grey diagonal overlay (avg RGB distance 48 vs 121-169 for the others); the others frequently report the grey overlay's hex instead.
- Recommendation: Sonnet as primary L1 analyser (matches the plan's existing default); Haiku only for screen-type triage on Latin-script decks, never for KO/TH text; gpt-6-astra as the L2 cross-family reviewer (only option, but earned it on the numbers too); Fable as an escalation tier for disputed/low-confidence band-colour or text reads, not a bulk default.
- Confirms/extends the plan's cost gotcha: a `claude -p` call without `--system-prompt-file` burns $0.05-0.53 on the default system-prompt cache alone before answering anything (hit `budget_exhausted` on all 4 models at a $0.05 cap); with a minimal custom system prompt, full vision-analysis calls cost $0.017-$0.16 including the answer.
- Also found: OpenAI's strict structured-output mode requires `additionalProperties:false` and every property in `required` at every schema level — plan-v1.md's schema note doesn't call this out yet.
- Bake-off decided the models (docs/research/vision-models-bakeoff.md): Sonnet 5 for L1, GPT-6 Astra for L2 (the only GPT model that works on the account), Fable 5.1 as escalation, Opus 5.5 for recommendations. No Haiku on Korean or Thai. Review page updated to v2. Waiting on the user's responses on the page.
